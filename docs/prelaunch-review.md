## Trademark clearance request

Search Quin Súper, Quin Super and QuinSuper, including phonetic/visual
similarities and relevant Catalan/Spanish variants. Start with candidate
classes 9, 35 and 42; counsel must confirm goods/services and search scope.

Cover:
- OEPM national marks and trade names.
- International registrations designating Spain.
- EU trade marks and relevant international registrations.
- Relevant app-store, business-name and unregistered-use checks.

Official starting points:
- https://consultas2.oepm.es/LocalizadorWeb/
- https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/search-ip

For each search retain date, query, filters, results/export, registration
numbers, owners, status, goods/services and counsel's conflict assessment.
A web search or domain availability does not establish trademark clearance.
